About the game

WordQuest is a relaxing word search puzzle game. Swipe across letters in any direction — across, down, diagonally, even backwards — to uncover hidden words, and travel through eight themed worlds as the boards grow larger and the vocabulary grows richer.

Features

  • 240 handcrafted levels across 8 themed worlds
  • A real difficulty curve — 7×7 grids and simple words at the start, 13×13 boards with all eight directions by the end
  • Daily Challenge — a new puzzle every day, with streak bonuses that grow the longer you keep it going
  • Three hint types for when a board refuses to give up its secrets
  • Fully offline — every puzzle is generated on your device, so it works in airplane mode
  • Achievements, stars and player ranks to chase
  • Light and dark themes, plus high-contrast and large-letter accessibility options

No account required. No sign-up. Your progress stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.
